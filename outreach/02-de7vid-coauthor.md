nuqneH! I'm reaching out to suggest a collaboration; something fun. I'm proposing a project to add a **Klingon (`tlh`) interface to the very hot set of Apps**, a new open-source suite (MIT OR Apache-2.0): WordCraft (word processor), PdfCraft, PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, FilmCraft, CADCraft, with spreadsheet and slides apps on the way. The upstream request is here: https://github.com/storytold/craft-i18n/issues/43.

All the apps share one set of translations, so Klingon done once shows up across the suite. We start with the shared strings and WordCraft (about 4,400 strings), then go app by app.

The hard part is a **suite glossary**: a few hundred core interface terms (file, save, layer, export, undo…), then smaller sets for documents, images, video and CAD. Every term built only from Okrand's words and word-building rules, and used the same way in every app.

Your boQwI' data and translation guidelines are the best foundation I know of for this, and its Apache-2.0 license matches the apps'. I'd love to have you as **co-author of the glossary**, with the final say on disputed terms. You'd be credited in craft-i18n's `ATTRIBUTION.md` and in our project.

Whatever level of involvement suits you is great:
- **Co-author:** shape and approve the glossary
- **Reviewer:** check the core glossary and a sample of strings
- **Advice:** point us at existing conventions or the right reviewers

Plan, glossary and status: https://github.com/CosmicCEO/klingon-localization

Questions or discussion are welcome here or in issues on that repo.

Qapla'!
